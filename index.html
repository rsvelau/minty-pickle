Players

    Players take turns. Each starts on the Start space and moves one cell per pip. It keeps going while there's one way forward and stops at forks: tap or click a round arrow button (or use the arrow keys). With Follow streets on, roads only go where they lead. Walls, windows, locked and one-way doors and blocked cells stop it; walking over a Key unlocks its building.

    Click or drag to place on walkable cells (Shift-drag fills a rectangle). Markers sit on top of a cell and never change it. Hover a Key to see the locked and one-way doors it unlocks in its building.

    Click a start cell, then hover any cell to see the shortest route; click to pin it. Click the start again (or press Esc) to clear. Blocked cells, walls, windows and locked doors stop movement; one-way doors only go their arrow's way. Walking onto a Key picks it up and unlocks its building's locked and one-way doors; routes fetch keys when needed.

    Drag along grid lines to draw. Click an edge to place or remove one piece; doors and storefront windows sit centred between two cells. For a one-way door, click on the side people come from (click from the other side to flip it). Walls, locked and one-way doors block light; unlocked doors and windows let it through. Rectangle draws a room outline (Erase clears everything inside it).

    Board size

    Scale: 1 cell ≈ 7 m (23 ft), the width of a narrow two-lane street with sidewalks. A small home is about 1.3 × 1.1 cells. Shrinking removes cells past the new edge; undo restores them.
